You are designing a web-based email client that looks like a page of well-set text, not a software interface.

VISUAL LANGUAGE
- Pure black text on pure white background. Grayscale only for secondary text (#888, #bbb). No other colors unless this prompt names one accent.
- One typeface family only (a refined humanist sans such as Inter or Söhne, OR a classic book serif such as Tiempos or Freight — choose one and use it everywhere). Two sizes maximum: body and a slightly larger heading.
- Generous whitespace. Content column is centered, about 65–75 characters wide. Large margins.
- Sender, subject, and time are quiet metadata in gray, set in the same typeface, not in columns.
- No icons except at most a single thin-line glyph where absolutely necessary.
- Feel: iA Writer, a printed letter, a well-designed book page. Calm, slow, intentional.

HARD CONSTRAINTS — do NOT include any of the following:
- No sidebar, no left navigation, no folder tree, no labels list
- No three-pane layout (list + message + sidebar)
- No top toolbar, no button bars, no formatting toolbar
- No cards, no boxes, no borders around messages, no drop shadows
- No avatars, no profile pictures, no colored circles with initials
- No unread counts, badges, red dots, or notification bubbles
- No checkboxes, stars, flags, or row hover buttons
- No tabs (Primary/Social/Promotions etc.)
- No search bar visible unless this prompt asks for it
- No gradients, no illustrations, no logos, no branding
- Nothing that looks like Gmail, Outlook, Superhuman, or Apple Mail

OUTPUT: a single desktop browser viewport, 1440×900, showing only the one screen described below.

THE SCREEN
This is the home screen at the moment of a mail delivery.

The page is mostly empty. Near the vertical center, set in the body size, two lines:

  Mail's here.
  3 need you, 14 to look over.

Beneath, two quiet text links set in the body size with generous spacing between them, no buttons or boxes:

  Start session
  Go to desk

Below that, a thin horizontal line spanning about half the page width representing today, with three small tick marks at 9:00, 1:00, and 5:00, and the current time shown as a small filled dot sitting exactly on the 1:00 tick. Times labeled in small gray text beneath the ticks.

Further down, show the three "need you" items as three short lines of text, like a table of contents: sender name in black, then subject in gray, on the same line. No list styling, no dividers, no bullets.

At the very bottom of the viewport, a single line of small gray text: "1 doorstep · 2 pile · 3 desk · 4 page · 5 drawer".

Nothing else on the page.
